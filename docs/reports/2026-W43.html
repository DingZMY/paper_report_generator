Molecular clockwork hypothesis for the KaiABC circadian oscillations.

Sasai Masaki · Fujishiro Shin

When three cyanobacterial proteins-KaiA, KaiB, and KaiC-are incubated with ATP in vitro, the phosphorylation level of KaiC exhibits stable circadian oscillations. Biochemical and structural analyses have shown that KaiC's ATPase activity is crucial for these oscillations, leading to the hypothesis that ATP-consuming dynamics function as a molecular clock, determining the oscillation period of individual molecules. Moreover, these molecular clocks synchronize collectively, resulting in oscillations at the ensemble level. In this study, we develop a theoretical model to test this molecular clockwork hypothesis. Our model clarifies the relationship between the oscillation period and ATPase activity, explaining the significant changes in the period induced by amino acid substitutions near the CI-CII domain boundary of the KaiC hexamer. Furthermore, the model addresses the physical basis for temperature compensation concerning both the oscillation period and ATPase activity. Thus, the molecular clockwork perspective provides a framework for understanding the atomic design behind collective oscillations.
当三种蓝藻蛋白——KaiA、KaiB 和 KaiC——在体外与 ATP 一起孵育时，KaiC 的磷酸化水平呈现稳定的昼夜节律振荡。生物化学和结构分析表明，KaiC 的 ATP 酶活性对这些振荡至关重要，由此提出假设：消耗 ATP 的动力学过程作为分子钟发挥作用，决定单个分子的振荡周期。此外，这些分子钟集体同步，从而在群体水平产生振荡。在本研究中，我们建立了一个理论模型来检验这一分子钟机制假说。该模型阐明了振荡周期与 ATP 酶活性之间的关系，解释了 KaiC 六聚体 CI-CII 结构域边界附近的氨基酸替换所诱导的周期显著变化。此外，该模型还探讨了振荡周期和 ATP 酶活性温度补偿的物理基础。因此，分子钟视角为理解集体振荡背后的原子设计提供了框架。

核心发现

  • 建立理论模型验证分子钟假说，阐明振荡周期与ATP酶活性的定量关系。
  • 解释了KaiC六聚体CI-CII结构域边界氨基酸替换导致周期显著变化的机制。
  • 揭示振荡周期和ATP酶活性温度补偿的物理基础，支持集体振荡的原子设计。

Unidirectional rotation in a molecular motor driven by angular-momentum transfer via helical orbitals.

Skolaut Julian · Marek Štěpán · Balzer Nico · Camarasa-Gómez María · Wilhelm Jan · Lukášek Jan · et al.

The generation of unidirectional motion has been a long-standing challenge in the engineering of molecular motors and, more generally, machines. A molecular motor is characterized by a set of low-energy states that differ in their configuration, that is, position or rotation. In biology and Feringa-type motors, unidirectional motion is driven by excitation of the molecule into a high-energy transitional state followed by a directional relaxation back to a low-energy state. Directionality is created by a steric hindrance to movement along one of the directions on the path from the excited state back to a low-energy state. Here we showcase a principal mechanism for the generation of unidirectional rotation of a molecule without the need for steric hindrance and transitional excited states. The chemical design of the molecule-consisting of a platform, an upright axle and a chiral rotor moiety-enables a rotation mechanism that relies on the transfer of orbital angular momentum from the driving current to the rotor. The transfer is mediated via orbital currents that are carried by helical orbitals in the axle.
在分子马达乃至更一般的机器工程中，单向运动的产生一直是一个长期挑战。分子马达的特征在于一组低能态，这些低能态在构型（即位置或旋转）上有所不同。在生物马达和费林加型马达中，单向运动是由分子激发到高能过渡态，随后定向弛豫回到低能态所驱动的。方向性是通过从激发态返回低能态路径上沿某一方向运动的空间位阻产生的。在此，我们展示了一种无需空间位阻和过渡激发态即可产生分子单向旋转的主要机制。该分子的化学设计——由平台、直立轴和手性转子部分组成——实现了一种旋转机制，该机制依赖于从驱动电流向转子转移轨道角动量。这一转移是通过轴中螺旋轨道所携带的轨道电流介导的。

核心发现

  • 提出无需空间位阻和过渡激发态的分子单向旋转新机制。
  • 分子由平台、直立轴和手性转子组成，实现轨道角动量从驱动电流向转子的转移。
  • 轨道角动量转移由轴中螺旋轨道携带的轨道电流介导。

Programmable and selective silencing of oncogenic fusion transcripts by CRISPR-Cas13b via breakpoint sequence recognition.

Hu 胡文欣 Wenxin · Chen 陈竑霖 Honglin · He Steven · Casan Joshua M L · Shembrey Carolyn · Li Sharon · et al.

Oncogenic gene fusions are key drivers of cancer, yet most remain undruggable by current therapies. Here, we establish CRISPR-PspCas13b as a personalizable platform for systematic silencing of various fusion transcripts. We reveal that recognition and cleavage of the breakpoint sequence by PspCas13b disrupt the fusion transcript, resulting in efficient protein depletion. PspCas13b cleavage produces aberrant, translation-incompetent target RNA species, a subset of which harbors internal deletions proximal to the cleavage site. Collectively, this study establishes a molecular framework for precise targeting of undruggable or drug-resistant oncogenic transcripts.

WNK1 links ionic signals to microtubule dynamics to control pyrin inflammasome assembly.

Lewis Genevieve · Mayes-Hopfinger Lindsey · Ali Md Kaisar · Xie Jian · Huang Chou-Long · Feng Hanping · et al.

WNK1 [with no lysine (K)] is a chloride- and potassium-sensitive kinase that regulates cellular ion homeostasis. Although WNK1 negatively regulates the NLRP3 and AIM2 inflammasomes, its role in pyrin inflammasome activation has remained unknown. Here, we identify WNK1 as a critical regulator of pyrin inflammasome assembly. In macrophages, genetic or pharmacologic inhibition of WNK1 suppresses pyrin activation without affecting pyrin dephosphorylation, demonstrating that WNK1 acts downstream of the canonical licensing step. This function occurs largely independently of the canonical SPAK/OXSR1 pathway. Mechanistically, WNK1 regulates microtubule dynamics: Its inhibition stabilizes microtubules, reduces plus-end growth, and impairs pyrin inflammasome assembly. Extracellular ionic conditions bidirectionally regulate this pathway, with hypotonic stress enhancing WNK1 activity and pyrin activation, whereas elevated extracellular potassium chloride suppresses WNK1 activity and inhibits pyrin assembly independently of osmotic stress. Together, these findings define an ion-sensitive WNK1-microtubule axis linking ionic signals to cytoskeletal dynamics to control pyrin inflammasome assembly and explain how WNK1 exerts opposing effects on distinct inflammasome pathways.

Methyltransferase RsmB chaperones E. coli ribosome assembly by orchestrating the recruitment of ribosomal proteins.

Wei Qilan · Woodson Sarah A

Ribosomal RNA modification enzymes increase cellular fitness, but it's little known whether this benefit arises from rRNA modification or a chaperone function of the modification enzyme. Here, we show that E. coli methyltransferase RsmB chaperones the initial recruitment of ribosomal proteins to the 16S 3' domain rRNA, a key step in assembly of the 30S subunit. Real-time single-molecule assembly experiments show that the RsmB product, 16S m5C967, has little effect on the rRNA affinity for ribosomal proteins. Instead, RsmB facilitates stable recruitment of primary protein uS7 during transcription while blocking the addition of secondary protein uS19. Completion of the methyltransferase cycle promotes RsmB release, allowing assembly to continue. Dimethyl sulfate footprinting of trapped RsmB-rRNA complexes revealed that RsmB restructures a critical rRNA helix junction. Our results illustrate how an RNA modification enzyme favors the proper sequence of protein addition by temporarily reshaping the energy landscape of co-transcriptional rRNA folding.

Latent effector capacity governs reversible T cell exhaustion: A theoretical mathematical model of PD-1 blockade response.

Liew Aubrey Y · Li Ying · Dong Haidong

T cell exhaustion is often viewed as a terminal state marked by irreversible loss of function during chronic infection and cancer. However, the rapid recovery of antitumor activity in clinical responders after PD-1 blockade suggests that some exhausted T cells retain hidden functional potential. To explain this paradox, we present a theoretical framework that separates latent effector capacity from active effector function. Latent effector capacity represents a history-dependent state reflecting preserved epigenetic accessibility and regulatory readiness, distinct from ongoing gene expression. In this model, PD-1 signaling acts as a reversible mask that suppresses effector function without eliminating the underlying effector capacity. The framework explains preserved chromatin accessibility, rapid reinvigoration after checkpoint blockade, and variable therapeutic responses. By incorporating nonlinear maintenance and erosion of latent effector capacity, the model predicts a bistable system with a point of no return beyond which exhaustion becomes irreversible. These principles may guide development of mechanistically informed biomarkers and artificial intelligence models predicting response to PD-1 therapy.

van Overbeek Nila K · Clijsters Linda · Wirth Martina · de Graaf Ilona J · de Lint Klaas · Uruci Sidrit · et al.

In this study, we sought to comprehensively map ubiquitin signaling networks at the crossroads of cellular proliferation and proteostasis, both crucial in cancer. Genome-wide CRISPR knockout screening in the presence of the ubiquitin E1 inhibitor TAK-243 identified 239 genes whose ablation sensitizes cells to ubiquitination blockade. Our findings uncover protein networks exhibiting synthetic lethality with ubiquitin signaling blockade and highlight vulnerabilities of actively proliferating cells to proteostatic imbalance at the chromatin.

Cheng Jen-Hao · Co Hannah K C · Lin Hao-Kuen · Wu Chia-Chou · Chen Sheng-Hong

We show that cellular self-organization constrains the propagation of ferroptosis, an iron-dependent form of oxidative cell death that can otherwise spread as unbounded trigger waves. Low-density, misaligned regions with elevated YAP/TEAD1 activity are sensitive, whereas high-density, aligned regions with increased NRF2 expression form propagation-resistant boundaries. Diabetes-mediated changes in red blood cell biomechanics reorganize capillary network hemodynamics through altered cellular trafficking.

LeCompte Shane · Pangilinan Patrickalan · Ding Yifu · Bedggood Phillip · Metha Andrew · Bagchi Prosenjit

Diabetes alters shape and deformability of red blood cells (RBCs), thereby potentially modifying their transport through capillary networks. Microvascular dysfunction-including impaired capillary perfusion, endothelial dysregulation, and vascular remodeling-is a hallmark of diabetes. However, the contribution of altered RBC biomechanics to network-scale changes in capillary hemodynamics and the cellular-scale mechanisms underlying such changes remain poorly understood. Current in vivo imaging techniques cannot directly quantify critical hemodynamic parameters including endothelial mechanical load, capillary pressure, and the cell-free layer in vessels less than 10 microns in diameter. Nor can they identify the cellular-scale mechanisms responsible for network-scale hemodynamic changes. To bridge these gaps, we combined anatomically reconstructed human retinal capillary networks with a high-fidelity three-dimensional fluid-structure interaction model that fully resolves RBC deformation and transport. The simulations predict that diabetes-mediated alterations in RBC shape and deformability redistribute capillary hemodynamics with bidirectional, highly localized, and spatially heterogeneous changes. Compared to healthy RBCs, diabetic RBCs resulted in hematocrit changes by ±40%, RBC velocity by -30% to +40%, vessel pressure drop by twofold, and local wall shear stress nearly fourfold. Diabetic RBCs also shifted the Fåhræus relationship where the concentration of cells decreases with vessel diameter through diameter-dependent changes in the cell-free layer. Analysis of individual bifurcations identified three cellular-scale mechanisms underlying these network-scale changes: reduced lingering, capillary obstruction, and slower lateral migration of diabetic RBCs that altered their partitioning into downstream vessels. Together, these mechanisms reorganized downstream cellular trafficking and redistributed capillary hemodynamics throughout the network. These findings establish how diabetes-mediated alterations in RBC biomechanics give rise to localized microvascular dysfunction by linking single-cell biomechanics to altered cellular trafficking and emergent capillary network hemodynamics.

Chovanec Peter · He Shiyang · Yin Yi

CRISPR/Cas9-induced DNA double-strand breaks (DSBs) are widely used for genome engineering, yet their capacity to provoke sister chromatid exchange (SCE) remains incompletely understood because exchanges between identical sisters leave no sequence change and are invisible to conventional sequencing.
