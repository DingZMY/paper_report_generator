## 1. Multi-omics-constrained kinetic models quantify pathway rewiring and predict metabolic vulnerabilities in BRCA1-deficient ovarian cancer.

| 字段 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **期刊** | Cell Syst |
| **发表日期** | 2026-Nov-13 |
| **作者** | Toumpe Ilias, Masid Maria, Hatzimanikatis Vassily, Miskovic Ljubisa |
| **原文链接** | [查看原文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cels.2026.101781) |
| **DOI** | `10.1016/j.cels.2026.101781` |

### 摘要（英文）

Time-resolved, network-wide consequences of cancer metabolic reprogramming remain poorly understood. We built multi-omics-constrained, near-genome-scale kinetic ensembles for isogenic BRCA1 wild-type and BRCA1-mutant ovarian cancer cells. Covering over 2,100 reactions, the ensembles enabled uncertainty-quantified simulations of nutrient, metabolite, and enzyme perturbations. Metabolic control analysis identified 28 enzyme-mediated vulnerabilities; 24 are linked to experimental or approved DrugBank compounds, whereas four lack documented drug associations. Independent DepMap data supported a subset of these candidates. All 28 emerged directly from the mechanistic model structure and multi-omics constraints, with no calibration to known drug targets or cancer-dependency data. Simulations of thymidylate synthase, hexokinase, and triokinase inhibition predicted target- and physiology-specific changes in pathway use, energy metabolism, and a shared ceramide-linked stress response. Mapping predicted enzyme-activity shifts to BRCA1-associated transcription factors suggested regulatory routes underlying differences between the two states. These ensembles provide a reusable framework for time-resolved, mechanistic hypothesis generation across cancers and other diseases. A record of this paper's transparent peer review process is included in the supplemental information.

## 2. Noncanonical enhancers control gene expression and cell fate in human pluripotent stem cells.

| 字段 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **期刊** | Sci Adv |
| **发表日期** | 2026-Nov-13 |

### 摘要（英文）

Enhancers are key gene regulatory elements that ensure the precise spatiotemporal execution of developmental gene expression programs. However, recent findings indicate that current approaches to identify enhancers may not capture the full repertoire of active enhancers in mammalian genomes. Here, we combine massively parallel enhancer assays with chromatin structure and transcriptome profiling to functionally annotate enhancers genome-wide in human induced pluripotent stem cells. We find that a substantial fraction (∼40%) of accessible chromatin regions with enhancer function lack key features associated with active enhancers, including the active enhancer mark histone H3 lysine-27 acetylation and enhancer-associated RNAs. Perturbation of this class of noncanonical enhancers by CRISPR-mediated epigenome editing or deletion results in decreased levels of target gene expression and, in one instance, loss of pluripotent stem cell characteristics. Collectively, our data demonstrate enhancer activity for a class of gene regulatory elements that had, until now, only been associated with a neutral or inactive status, challenging current models of enhancer function.

## 3. Symmetry as a common origin of hierarchical emergence and abstraction.

| 字段 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **期刊** | Sci Adv |
| **发表日期** | 2026-Nov-13 |
| **作者** | Rosas Fernando E |
| **原文链接** | [查看原文](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.aeh0773) |
| **DOI** | `10.1126/sciadv.aeh0773` |

### 摘要（英文）

Many systems of interest exhibit nested emergent layers with their own rules and regularities, and our knowledge about them seems naturally organized around these levels. How do such hierarchical structures arise, and why should observers track these hierarchies so closely? This paper proposes symmetry as an answer to both questions. By combining principles from information theory, group theory, and statistical mechanics, it is shown that dynamical processes that are equivariant with respect to a symmetry group give rise to multiple emergent macroscopic levels arranged according to the algebraic structure of the symmetry. The same symmetries shape the Bayesian beliefs of an observer with only partial access to the system, yielding a matching hierarchy of "abstract" beliefs that can be updated autonomously at different levels of resolution. These ideas are illustrated in Hopfield networks, where familiar macroscopic quantities emerge from known symmetries, and in Boolean models of gene regulation, where symmetries are instead found from the dynamics. The computational advantages provided by symmetry are showcased in Ehrenfest diffusion and epidemic modeling, where abstract beliefs enable Bayesian inference over exponentially large state spaces. These results identify symmetry as a sufficient mechanism for hierarchical emergence and-at the same time-for the capacity of abstract beliefs to mirror the systems they track, making feasible inferential problems that would otherwise be intractable.

### 摘要（英文）

Chromosomal instability (CIN) is an established driver of aneuploidy and cell death. Here, we investigate the role of apoptosis as a barrier against CIN-driven aneuploidy in B lymphocytes. We found that impairment of their spindle assembly checkpoint via deletion of Mitotic Arrest Deficient 2-Like 1 or mutation of Monopolar Spindle 1 kinase curtails B cell survival. A CRISPR screen identified intermediates of mitochondrial apoptosis as critical, which we confirmed in vivo by overexpression of a B cell-restricted BCL2 transgene, outperforming loss of P53. In contrast, high cell death rates rapidly select for specific karyotypes, facilitating aneuploid tumor cell survival in Eμ-Myc transgenic mice. Hence, cell death inhibition fails to allow CIN-driven aneuploidies to prevail. In contrast, BCL2-regulated apoptosis selects viable aneuploid tumor karyotypes in the presence of oncogenic MYC, a notion with implications for the clinical use of BCL2-targeting therapies.

### 摘要（英文）

How semigamy generates haploids during double fertilization has remained elusive for more than six decades. Here, we identify Gamete Expressed 1 (GEX1) as the gene underlying semigamy and show that it mediates dual-origin haploid induction in cotton. A naturally occurring mutation in GEX1 disrupts normal nuclear membrane fusion during fertilization, resulting in delayed or incomplete karyogamy. Cytological analyses reveal that delayed karyogamy allows the closely apposed but unfused gametic nuclei to undergo independent mitotic divisions, giving rise to maternal haploids, paternal haploids, and chimeric progeny. CRISPR-Cas9-mediated disruption of GEX1 reproduced the characteristic defects in nuclear fusion and enabled both maternal and paternal haploid induction within a single generation. Together, our findings uncover a genetically tractable mechanism underlying semigamy that links nuclear membrane fusion dynamics to haploid formation and establish a versatile strategy for rapidly generating doubled haploids from both parental genomes to accelerate crop breeding.

## 7. Transforming macromolecular structures into simulations of self-assembly with ioNERDSS.

| 字段 | 内容 |
|---|---|
| **期刊** | Biophys J |
| **发表日期** | 2026-Nov-12 |
| **作者** | Ying Yue Moon, Sang Mankun, Au Gabriel, Chhibber Smriti, Du Yufeng, Fischer Jonathan A, et al. |
| **原文链接** | [查看原文](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bpj.2026.11.011) |
| **DOI** | `10.1016/j.bpj.2026.11.011` |

### 摘要（英文）

Macromolecular self-assembly is a fundamental process in living and engineered systems, producing molecular machines like the ribosome or highly symmetric viral capsids. Thanks to sources like the Protein Data Bank (PDB) and AlphaFold3, the final target complexes are often known, but these static structures do not provide information on the self-assembly process directly. Computational models provide critical tools to study these essential pathways of self-assembly, but substantial coarse graining of assembly subunits is necessary to achieve computational tractability of these relatively slow processes while retaining multi-valency. While local interactions overcome the often-prohibitive enumeration of all possible assembly intermediates, they must ensure global structural constraints are met. We here demonstrate ioNERDSS, a user-friendly Python package that transforms 3D atomic protein structures into coarse-grained (CG) models for immediate simulation with the stochastic reaction-diffusion NERDSS software, converting static structures into time-resolved assembly trajectories. With ioNERDSS, each protein chain is defined by a rigid subunit with discrete interfaces and explicit orientational constraints that enforce a structured assembly. Repeated subunits (such as in viral capsids) are regularized to preserve the target topology across distinct stochastic assembly pathways, supporting assembly of structures with thousands of subunits. We initialize pairwise binding affinities using ML prediction tools, and our default CG models are all constrained by thermodynamic reversibility to reach an equilibrium steady-state. The binding rates and subunit abundances necessary to perform simulations are initialized at default values but represent key variables that users should tune to test hypotheses on assembly pathways or to optimize models to experimental datasets. Benchmarking on over 50,000 PDB structures shows the majority of CG models will stochastically assemble into target structures. The ioNERDSS Python library links with open-source tools to facilitate fast and user-friendly structure validation and analysis of thermodynamic, kinetic, and nonequilibrium drivers of macromolecular self-assembly.

### 摘要（中文）

大分子自组装是生命系统和工程系统中的基本过程，可产生核糖体或高度对称的病毒衣壳等分子机器。借助蛋白质数据银行（PDB）和 AlphaFold3 等来源，最终目标复合物通常是已知的，但这些静态结构并不能直接提供自组装过程的信息。计算模型为研究这些关键的自组装途径提供了重要工具，但需要对组装亚基进行大量粗粒化，才能在保持多价性的同时，使这些相对缓慢的过程在计算上可处理。局部相互作用虽然避免了通常难以实现的对所有可能组装中间体的枚举，但必须确保满足全局结构约束。我们在此展示了 ioNERDSS，一个用户友好的 Python 包，它将三维原子蛋白质结构转换为粗粒化（CG）模型，以便用随机反应-扩散软件 NERDSS 立即进行模拟，从而将静态结构转化为时间分辨的组装轨迹。利用 ioNERDSS，每条蛋白质链被定义为一个刚性亚基，具有离散界面和明确的取向约束，以强制实现有序组装。重复亚基（如病毒衣壳中的亚基）经过正则化，以在不同的随机组装途径中保持目标拓扑，支持包含数千个亚基的结构组装。我们使用机器学习预测工具初始化成对结合亲和力，默认的 CG 模型均受热力学可逆性约束，以到达平衡稳态。执行模拟所需的结合速率和亚基丰度以默认值初始化，但它们是用户应调节的关键变量，以检验组装途径假说或根据实验数据集优化模型。对超过 50,000 个 PDB 结构的基准测试表明，大多数 CG 模型能够随机组装成目标结构。ioNERDSS Python 库与开源工具连接，便于快速、用户友好地进行结构验证，并分析大分子自组装的热力学、动力学和非平衡驱动因素。


### 核心发现


- 开发ioNERDSS，将静态原子结构自动转化为可模拟的粗粒化模型，实现时间分辨自组装轨迹。

- 利用离散界面、取向约束和亚基正则化，在随机组装中保持目标拓扑，支持数千亚基复合物。

- 基准测试超5万个PDB结构，多数CG模型能组装成目标结构，并可分析热力学和动力学驱动因素。

### 摘要（英文）

The basal ganglia (BG) are conserved brain regions essential for motor control, learning, emotion, and cognition and are implicated in neurological and psychiatric disease, yet a unified cross-species taxonomy of BG cell types is lacking. Cis-regulatory analysis reveals conserved sequence grammars that encode cell identity and inform viral targeting, and cell-type-resolved mapping localizes psychiatric and neurodegenerative risk to specific BG cell populations. This work provides a foundational resource for BG evolution, function, and disease.
